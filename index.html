How to click through

  1. Home → Credit Card Bill.
  2. Pay the HDFC bill, enter any 4-digit PIN.
  3. Success screen shows the card’s goals and a tip.
  4. Track all my cards → pick the variant → Agree.
  5. Card Goals dashboard, with Estimated / Not enough data labels.
  6. A lounge alert pops up; tap it, or try the checkout: pick a flight, train, bus or movie and see the tagged card’s progress move.
